# Barry's Beans — Gamified Loyalty App Prototype

MA UX prototyping project. A gamified coffee loyalty app where customers enter a daily code from their cup, smush falling beans Guitar Hero-style, and compete for a weekly free coffee.

## Screens

1. **Code Entry** — Enter the unique code printed on your Barry's cup
2. **Smush Game** — Tap to smush falling beans as they hit the target zone
3. **Leaderboard** — Weekly rankings, top scorer wins a free coffee
4. **Winner** — Redeemable coupon for the weekly champion
